The International Oceanic Association published its annual report detailing temperature trends across the Pacific Ocean. Satellite data collected between 2020 and 2025 indicates a baseline rise of 0.4 degrees Celsius in sea surface temperatures. Researchers used statistical algorithms to model sea ice volume changes in the Arctic region over the next two decades.

The study gathered measurements from 120 oceanographic buoys deployed near Australia, Japan, and Canada. Project Lead Dr. Robert Vance emphasized that continuous environmental monitoring remains essential for meteorological forecasting and marine ecosystem conservation.
